BUILDING DATA VALIDATION · 16 OCTOBER 2026

What is known, estimated and still unverified?

A five-council desktop audit of 122,414 building footprints, including the 2,573 apartment candidates currently used by HEATSCAPE.

This audit adds provenance and official-record associations. It is not a complete verified apartment register, an occupancy audit or an independent height-accuracy assessment. The scoring dataset and its 188 consistent-top result are unchanged.

Heights: origin is not accuracy

  • 1,208 candidate heights: Microsoft machine-learning estimates.
  • 361: estimated from floor counts (approximately 3.32 m per floor).
  • 351: filled using a group median.
  • 510: City of Melbourne model extrusion heights.
  • 143: other Overture source-reported heights.

The first three groups total 1,920 (74.6%). Source-reported heights are not automatically surveyed measurements. The provenance of the height actually used takes precedence over an unused Overture height attached to the same feature. Height uncertainty affects building-scale scores and shadow modelling.

Apartment evidence and possible omissions

All current candidates were selected by the Overture apartments class. Additional checks produced 274 unique CLUE apartment-property matches, 48 ambiguous/proximity property associations, 38 completed-project associations, 27 planning/construction associations only, and 2186 candidates without apartment corroboration in the retrieved official records.

Outside the candidate set, 1336 footprints have CLUE apartment-property associations and 187 others intersect completed apartment sites. A further 1821 weak leads use class/size rules. There are 114 unmatched official apartment locations/sites. These queues can contain non-residential parts, podiums or ambiguous locations. They are not added to the ranking.

80,092 source footprints have no Overture class. Missing labels and uneven council coverage prevent a complete recall estimate or a credible count of “all missing apartments”. No matching evidence is not evidence of non-residential use.

52 current candidates need a use review because a uniquely matched CLUE property reports a different predominant use. This is a review flag, not a finding that the apartment label is wrong: mixed use, building parts and source dates require investigation.

Requested attributes: obtained and missing

For Melbourne’s 510 candidates, unique property-point matches provide a construction-year value for 272, refurbishment year for 40 and dwelling-type/count records for 293. These are dated property records. Refurbishment blanks mean unknown; household counts are not the same as dwelling counts. CLUE distinguishes residential and student apartments; serviced accommodation cannot be consistently resolved from these datasets.

No equivalent complete building-level age, refurbishment or dwelling extract was obtained for Maribyrnong, Yarra, Port Phillip or Stonnington. UDP supplies project-level counts and maximum storeys across all five councils, not building-level replacements. No building-linked energy ratings, equipment inventory or full independent height reference was obtained. Current policy standards do not establish the energy performance of existing buildings.

Worked example: Swanston Central

COM_819350 uniquely intersects the CLUE 2024 property point for property 644814, address 156–172 Victoria Street, Carlton. CLUE reports Residential Apartment, construction year 2020, 72 floors and 1,045 residential apartments; refurbishment year is missing. These values are shown as reported, with the survey date. The model continues to use its council extrusion height of 238.6 m and its original Overture floor value of 71; CLUE floors are supporting evidence, not a replacement height measurement.

Methods and limits

clue. EPSG:7855. Point-in-footprint; multiple footprints retained as ambiguous. If none, nearest footprint within 5 m is proximity only. Dwellings join by CLUE property ID; never sum counts over matched footprints.

udp. 2022 and 2025 snapshots only; latest snapshot for each project ID. Associate only if at least 50% of footprint area intersects project polygon in its council. Retain actual overlap; no legal-use certification.

omissions. A review queue, not confirmed missing apartments. Separate property evidence, completed-site associations and weak size/class heuristics. Do not add these records to rankings.

height. Origin of the value actually used. Council model takes precedence over unused Overture height. Microsoft height is an ML estimate even when the original file says SOURCE_HEIGHT. Floors convert at approximately 3.32 m per floor.

limits. No site visits, occupancy-certificate audit, or independent complete height ground truth. No address-level energy ratings/equipment obtained. Policy definitions are review criteria, not evidence that a particular building complies.

failedSources. UDP WFS attempts for 2021, 2023 and 2024 returned HTTP 400. The WFS capability catalogue was then checked: it advertised mrs2016, mrs2022 and mrs2025. The 2016 dataset is explicitly marked obsolete by DataVic and was not used. The retrieved 2022/2025 pair is not a continuous completion register; project status is dated to its snapshot, not asserted current in 2026.

All source geometries are valid and all building IDs unique in the current inventory. Earlier candidate rules flagged 135 features; source-overlap and shared-ID flags can indicate tower/podium parts rather than duplicate buildings. The crosscheck adds differing-use, ambiguous-match and floor-discrepancy flags. No automatic reclassification or deduplication is performed.

Sources and policy context

  • Overture buildings: schema and provenance

    All five councils; project snapshot 2026-08-19.0, not the latest release. OSM apartment tags are selection evidence, not verified legal use. The project export has no construction/refurbishment year, dwelling-unit count or energy rating. Floors are not dwelling counts.

    Overture sources retain their original licences; OSM / Microsoft records here use ODbL.
  • City of Melbourne 2023 building footprints

    Melbourne geometry and model extrusion heights already used by the system. This is source provenance, not an independent height check. Tower / podium components may overlap.

    See dataset licence; City of Melbourne attribution retained.
  • CLUE buildings: age, floors and predominant use

    2024 survey; City of Melbourne only. Property-level points. Age/use/floors may be missing or ambiguous against multiple footprints.

    CC BY 4.0, City of Melbourne.
  • CLUE residential dwellings

    2024 survey; Melbourne property ID and dwelling type/count. Counts are not allocated to individual footprints. Student apartments remain a distinct type.

    CC BY 4.0, City of Melbourne.
  • Victorian Urban Development Program 2022

    Major residential redevelopment project polygons, including all five councils. Not a complete inventory of existing apartment buildings.

    CC BY 4.0, Victorian Government.
  • Victorian Urban Development Program 2025

    Latest of the two retrieved project snapshots wins for a repeated project ID. Project apartment counts, maximum storeys and completion year are not building attributes.

    CC BY 4.0, Victorian Government.
  • UDP methods and coverage

    Projects with 10 or more dwellings. Small or older apartments can be absent. Proposed / under-construction sites are not treated as completed.

  • Victorian Building and Plumbing Commission: building classes

    Class 2, Class 3 and mixed-use distinctions guide review. Overture labels and height alone do not establish an NCC class or distinguish all serviced / student accommodation.

  • Vicmap Buildings: official 3D coverage and access

    Coverage and level of detail vary. No independent, complete, downloadable five-council height reference was obtained for this audit; height accuracy remains unvalidated.

  • Building approval and end-of-project checks

    Planning approval is not proof of completed construction or occupied use. Individual occupancy / building records remain a follow-up.

  • NatHERS existing-home assessment guidance

    Assessment / certificate evidence is needed. No building-linked energy ratings or equipment inventory were obtained across the five councils; none are inferred from age, roof shape or policy standards.

  • Maribyrnong planning services and register

    Individual planning follow-up; not a bulk building-age, dwelling or occupancy dataset. No additional complete building-attribute extract was obtained.

  • Yarra planning permits and application search

    Individual planning follow-up. No complete building-age / dwelling / energy extract was obtained.

  • Stonnington planning register

    Individual planning follow-up; application status is not occupied building use. No complete building-attribute extract was obtained.

  • Port Phillip planning documentation search

    Plans may need an individual request. The open-data catalogue lists council-asset sustainability datasets, not a complete private-apartment energy inventory.

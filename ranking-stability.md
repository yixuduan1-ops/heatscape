# Ranking stability

Default suite: six outer H/I/B choices × three extents × three internal H blends × three internal I blends = 162 tests. Each internal dimension tests 60/40, 70/30, 80/20. H pairs recent/historical temperature; I pairs low-income share/area rent-to-income. Source dates stay fixed.

Custom outer shares and custom internal shares are appended to their dimension; every combination is evaluated. One custom internal share gives 216 tests, two give 288; custom outer shares alone give 189. Clicking a test applies both internal shares, outer weights and radius. Reset restores outer 40/40/20 and internal 70/30 for each indicator.

Every default test uses the same 2,560-building cohort. Top 20% requests 512 positions, with ties included. There are 138 buildings in all 162 settings; Swanston Central is selected in 153/162, rank 9–671. The default live result remains 81.5, rank 54/2,561. The complete-test and live cohorts differ.

Missing evidence is not a failed selection. List/map filters never change reference curves or ranks. Heat-quality flags are diagnostic, not additional exclusions. See [full model](model.html).

# Ranking stability

Six outer H/I/B shares × three extents × three recent/historical heat blends = 54 tests; custom shares add nine. The fixed multi-day summary never changes source dates. The tested blends are 60/40, 70/30 and 80/20; default 70/30.

Every test ranks the same complete-evidence cohort. Missing evidence has null selection and rank, not zero selection. Percentage size uses ceil(cohort × share); ties at cutoff enter. Common cohort 2,560; default Top 20% = 512 requested positions, 160 buildings selected in every test. Swanston Central 51/54, rank 11–664. Live 100 m ranks 2,561 candidates and requests 513 positions.

List/map filters do not change reference curves or ranks. Clicking a cell applies its weights, extent and blend; Reset restores 40/40/20 and 70/30. This is sensitivity to assumptions, not a validation against indoor conditions, actual incomes or energy outcomes. See [full model](model.html).

Heat H uses 70% recent LST percentile and 30% equal-mean historical temperature percentiles by default. Tested blends: 60/40, 70/30 and 80/20. Every one of the 54 combinations is displayed. 50 m is exploratory; support and concentration flags do not create additional score exclusions.
